# New FK perturbations frozen before exact validation

160 Hamiltonians: four original couplings, five independent frozen patterns, four amplitudes, and two distinct perturbation classes. Each case compares contact construction, physical prior, frozen columns, canonical k8/k16/k32 at 16 and 32 actual terms, plus multistart HFB and adaptive energy-residual columns. All 2400 predictions must finish and be hashed before new exact diagonalization.

The full machine-readable protocol, seeds, source costs, limitations and success criteria are in preregistration.json. Canonical candidates use the coefficient measure Lambda; the historical source named it P. This experiment does not silently reinterpret it as overlap^(2k) sampling.
